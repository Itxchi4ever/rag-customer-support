TECHNOVA GENERAL FAQ

Q1. How long is the TechNova product warranty?

TechNova products include a limited 2-year warranty from
the date of purchase.

Q2. How long do I have to request a return?

Eligible products can generally be returned within 14 days
of delivery.

Q3. How long does a refund take?

Approved refunds are generally processed within 5-7 business
days after the return is approved.

Q4. How long does standard shipping take?

Standard delivery generally takes 3-5 business days after
the order has been dispatched.

Q5. How do I contact customer support?

Customers can contact TechNova Customer Support for
assistance with product, warranty, return, refund, shipping,
and technical issues.

Q6. Is accidental damage covered by warranty?

Accidental damage is not covered under the standard
TechNova warranty.

Q7. Can I replace a defective product?

Eligible defective products may qualify for replacement
after inspection.

Q8. How do I reset my SmartWatch?

A SmartWatch can be restarted by pressing and holding the
power button for approximately 10 seconds. A factory reset
is available through Settings → System → Factory Reset.

Q9. My SmartWatch is not charging. What should I do?

Check the charging cable, magnetic alignment, and charging
contacts. Clean the contacts and try another compatible
power adapter.

Q10. My TechBuds are not connecting.

Check Bluetooth, keep the earbuds close to the smartphone,
and try pairing them again. If necessary, remove the earbuds
from Bluetooth settings and pair them again.
