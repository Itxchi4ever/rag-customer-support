TECHNOVA REFUND POLICY

1. REFUND ELIGIBILITY

Customers may request a refund for eligible products
within 14 days of delivery.

2. CONDITION

The product should be returned in reasonable condition
with its original accessories and packaging where possible.

3. REFUND PROCESS

After receiving and inspecting the returned product,
TechNova will process the approved refund.

4. REFUND TIME

Approved refunds are generally processed within 5-7
business days after the return is approved.

5. NON-ELIGIBLE ITEMS

Products damaged through misuse or unauthorized modification
may not qualify for a refund.

6. CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Contact TechNova Customer Support if you need assistance
with a refund request.
