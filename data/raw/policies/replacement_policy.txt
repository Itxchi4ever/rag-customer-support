TECHNOVA REPLACEMENT POLICY

Customers may request replacement of an eligible defective
product.

A replacement request should be submitted within the
applicable warranty or return period.

TechNova may inspect the product before approving a
replacement.

If the product is confirmed to have a manufacturing defect,
TechNova may provide a replacement according to company
policy.

Damage caused by misuse, unauthorized repairs, or accidents
may not qualify for replacement.

Contact TechNova Customer Support to start a replacement
request.
