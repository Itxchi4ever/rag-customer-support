TECHNOVA RETURN POLICY

Customers may request a return within 14 days of delivery
for eligible products.

The product should be returned with its accessories and
original packaging where possible.

The returned product may be inspected before the return
is approved.

Products damaged through misuse may not be eligible for
return.

Customers should contact TechNova Customer Support before
sending a product back.

Return instructions will be provided after the request
is reviewed.
