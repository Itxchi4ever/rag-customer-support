TECHNOVA SHIPPING POLICY

1. STANDARD SHIPPING

Standard delivery generally takes 3-5 business days after
the order has been dispatched.

2. ORDER PROCESSING

Orders are generally processed within 1-2 business days.

3. ORDER TRACKING

Customers can track an order using the tracking information
provided after the order has been dispatched.

4. DELIVERY DELAYS

Delivery times may be affected by weather, holidays,
transportation problems, or other circumstances outside
TechNova's control.

5. SHIPPING SUPPORT

Customers should contact TechNova Customer Support if a
package has not arrived within the expected delivery period.
