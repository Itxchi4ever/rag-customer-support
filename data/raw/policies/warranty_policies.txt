TECHNOVA WARRANTY POLICY

1. WARRANTY PERIOD

TechNova products include a limited 2-year warranty
from the date of purchase.

2. WARRANTY COVERAGE

The warranty covers manufacturing defects and hardware
failures that occur during normal use.

3. NOT COVERED

The warranty does not cover:

- Accidental damage.
- Liquid damage.
- Unauthorized repairs.
- Physical damage caused by misuse.
- Normal cosmetic wear.

4. WARRANTY CLAIM

To make a warranty claim, the customer must provide
proof of purchase and product details.

TechNova may inspect the product before approving
a warranty claim.

5. SUPPORT

Customers can contact TechNova Customer Support for
assistance with warranty claims.
