TECHNOVA SMARTWATCH
USER MANUAL

Product Name: TechNova SmartWatch
Model: TN-W1

1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW

The TechNova SmartWatch is a wearable device designed
for fitness tracking, notifications, time management,
and everyday activity monitoring.

The device includes a touchscreen display, heart-rate
monitor, step counter, Bluetooth connectivity, and
rechargeable battery.

2. GETTING STARTED

To turn on the SmartWatch, press and hold the power
button for approximately 3 seconds.

To turn off the SmartWatch, press and hold the power
button and select the Power Off option on the screen.

3. CHARGING

The TechNova SmartWatch uses a magnetic charging cable.

To charge the device:

1. Connect the charging cable to a compatible power adapter.
2. Align the magnetic connector with the charging contacts
   on the back of the SmartWatch.
3. Ensure that the charging contacts are clean and dry.
4. Allow the device to charge.

A full charge may take approximately 2 hours.

4. BLUETOOTH CONNECTION

The SmartWatch connects to a smartphone through Bluetooth.

To connect:

1. Turn on Bluetooth on your smartphone.
2. Open the TechNova mobile application.
3. Select Add Device.
4. Select TechNova SmartWatch.
5. Follow the instructions shown in the application.

5. RESETTING THE DEVICE

To restart the SmartWatch, press and hold the power button
for approximately 10 seconds.

A factory reset can be performed through:

Settings → System → Factory Reset

A factory reset removes locally stored data from the device.

6. BATTERY

The SmartWatch contains a rechargeable lithium-ion battery.

Battery life depends on usage, screen brightness,
notifications, Bluetooth usage, and other settings.

For better battery life, reduce screen brightness and
disable unnecessary notifications.

7. SAFETY

Do not expose the SmartWatch or charging connector to
excessive heat.

Keep the charging connector clean and dry.

Do not attempt to open or repair the battery yourself.

8. CUSTOMER SUPPORT

If the SmartWatch continues to malfunction after following
the troubleshooting instructions, contact TechNova Customer
Support for further assistance.
