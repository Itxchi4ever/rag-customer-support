TECHNOVA SMARTWATCH
TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

1. SMARTWATCH DOES NOT TURN ON

If the SmartWatch does not turn on:

1. Press and hold the power button for 10 seconds.
2. Connect the SmartWatch to its magnetic charger.
3. Allow the device to charge for at least 30 minutes.
4. Try turning the device on again.

If the SmartWatch still does not turn on, contact
TechNova Customer Support.

2. SMARTWATCH IS NOT CHARGING

If the SmartWatch is not charging:

1. Check that the charging cable is connected correctly.
2. Make sure the magnetic connector is aligned properly.
3. Clean the charging contacts with a dry, soft cloth.
4. Try a different compatible power adapter.
5. Allow the device to charge for at least 30 minutes.

If the device still does not charge, contact TechNova
Customer Support.

3. BLUETOOTH CONNECTION PROBLEM

If the SmartWatch cannot connect to your smartphone:

1. Make sure Bluetooth is enabled on your smartphone.
2. Keep the SmartWatch within Bluetooth range.
3. Restart Bluetooth on your smartphone.
4. Restart the SmartWatch.
5. Open the TechNova mobile application and try pairing
   the device again.

4. BATTERY DRAINS QUICKLY

If the battery drains faster than expected:

1. Reduce screen brightness.
2. Disable unnecessary notifications.
3. Reduce continuous heart-rate monitoring if not required.
4. Ensure that the SmartWatch software is up to date.

5. FACTORY RESET

If other troubleshooting methods do not resolve the issue,
a factory reset may be performed through:

Settings → System → Factory Reset

Remember that a factory reset removes locally stored data.

6. WHEN TO CONTACT SUPPORT

Contact TechNova Customer Support if:

- The SmartWatch does not turn on after charging.
- The device does not charge after trying another compatible
  charger.
- Bluetooth pairing continues to fail.
- The screen is damaged.
- The device becomes unusually hot.
