TECHNOVA TECHBOOK
USER MANUAL

Product Name: TechNova TechBook
Model: TN-L1

1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW

The TechNova TechBook is a laptop designed for everyday
computing, productivity, education, and entertainment.

2. GETTING STARTED

Press the power button to turn on the laptop.

Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the initial
system setup.

3. CHARGING

Connect the supplied power adapter to the charging port.

The charging indicator shows the charging status.

Use the supplied or compatible TechNova power adapter.

4. BATTERY

Battery life depends on screen brightness, applications,
network connections, and system settings.

For improved battery life:

- Reduce screen brightness.
- Close unnecessary applications.
- Disable unused wireless connections.
- Use battery-saving mode when appropriate.

5. CONNECTIVITY

The TechBook supports Wi-Fi and Bluetooth connectivity.

To connect to Wi-Fi:

1. Open network settings.
2. Select the desired Wi-Fi network.
3. Enter the network password.
4. Select Connect.

6. SYSTEM RESTART

To restart the TechBook:

1. Open the system menu.
2. Select Power.
3. Select Restart.

7. CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Contact TechNova Customer Support if the laptop continues
to malfunction after following the troubleshooting guide.
