TECHNOVA TECHBOOK
TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

1. LAPTOP DOES NOT TURN ON

If the TechBook does not turn on:

1. Connect the power adapter.
2. Check that the charging indicator is active.
3. Allow the laptop to charge for at least 30 minutes.
4. Press and hold the power button for 10 seconds.
5. Try turning the laptop on again.

If the laptop still does not turn on, contact TechNova
Customer Support.

2. LAPTOP IS RUNNING SLOWLY

Try the following:

1. Close unnecessary applications.
2. Restart the laptop.
3. Check available storage.
4. Install available system updates.
5. Restart the laptop after updates are installed.

3. WI-FI IS NOT WORKING

1. Check that Wi-Fi is enabled.
2. Restart Wi-Fi.
3. Restart the laptop.
4. Restart the wireless router.
5. Try connecting to another network.

4. BATTERY DRAINS QUICKLY

Reduce screen brightness and close applications that
are not required.

Enable battery-saving mode.

If battery performance remains unusually low, contact
TechNova Customer Support.

5. LAPTOP IS OVERHEATING

Ensure that ventilation openings are not blocked.

Use the laptop on a hard, flat surface.

Close applications that use significant processing power.

If the laptop becomes unusually hot, stop using it and
contact TechNova Customer Support.
