TECHNOVA TECHBUDS
USER MANUAL

Product Name: TechNova TechBuds
Model: TN-B1

1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW

TechNova TechBuds are wireless earbuds designed for
music, calls, and everyday use.

The earbuds support Bluetooth connectivity and include
a charging case.

2. GETTING STARTED

Place both earbuds inside the charging case.

Open the charging case and remove the earbuds.

The earbuds automatically enter pairing mode when used
for the first time.

3. BLUETOOTH PAIRING

To connect the TechBuds:

1. Open the charging case.
2. Enable Bluetooth on your smartphone.
3. Open Bluetooth settings.
4. Select "TechNova TechBuds".
5. Confirm the pairing request.

Once paired, the earbuds can automatically reconnect
to the previously connected device.

4. CHARGING

Place both earbuds inside the charging case.

Connect the charging case to a compatible USB charging
adapter using the supplied charging cable.

A complete charge may take approximately 1.5 hours.

5. CONTROLS

Tap the right earbud once to play or pause media.

Double-tap the right earbud to play the next track.

Tap the left earbud once to answer an incoming call.

6. BATTERY

Battery life depends on volume level, usage, Bluetooth
connection, and other settings.

Keep the earbuds inside the charging case when they
are not being used.

7. SAFETY

Keep the earbuds and charging case away from excessive
moisture.

Do not attempt to open or repair the earbuds yourself.

8. CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Contact TechNova Customer Support if the earbuds continue
to malfunction after following the troubleshooting guide.
