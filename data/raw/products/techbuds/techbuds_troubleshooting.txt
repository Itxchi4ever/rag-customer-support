TECHNOVA TECHBUDS
TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

1. TECHBUDS ARE NOT CONNECTING

If the TechBuds do not connect:

1. Make sure Bluetooth is enabled.
2. Keep the earbuds close to the smartphone.
3. Place both earbuds inside the charging case.
4. Remove the earbuds and try pairing again.
5. Restart Bluetooth on the smartphone.

If the problem continues, remove "TechNova TechBuds"
from the phone's Bluetooth settings and pair them again.

2. ONLY ONE EARBUD IS WORKING

Place both earbuds inside the charging case.

Wait for approximately 10 seconds and remove them again.

Make sure both earbuds have sufficient battery.

If the issue continues, reset the earbuds and pair them
with the smartphone again.

3. EARBUDS ARE NOT CHARGING

Check that:

- The earbuds are correctly placed in the charging case.
- The charging contacts are clean.
- The charging case has sufficient battery.
- The charging cable is properly connected.

4. SOUND QUALITY IS POOR

Check the following:

1. Increase the smartphone volume.
2. Make sure the earbuds are positioned correctly.
3. Keep the smartphone within Bluetooth range.
4. Disconnect and reconnect the earbuds.

5. RESETTING THE TECHBUDS

To reset the TechBuds:

1. Place both earbuds inside the charging case.
2. Keep the case open.
3. Press and hold the case button for approximately
   10 seconds.
4. The indicator will flash to confirm the reset.

After resetting, pair the TechBuds with the smartphone again.

6. WHEN TO CONTACT SUPPORT

Contact TechNova Customer Support if:

- An earbud does not turn on.
- The earbuds do not charge.
- Bluetooth pairing continues to fail.
- The charging case does not charge.
- The device becomes unusually hot.
